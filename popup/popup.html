Colour, size and font here are the defaults for new notes — select a note on the page to restyle it. Only your writing is drawn: the overlay itself stays fully transparent (background above 0% adds a faint panel).

Toggle from the toolbar icon or Alt+Shift+O.

Open brave://extensions/shortcuts and find “Overlay Ink”.

Click anywhere on the page to place a note, drag its ⠯ grip to move it. In Edit mode the page pauses so clicks land in your notes; in View mode they stay visible while the page works normally.

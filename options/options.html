Overlay Ink

Transparent notes you can drop anywhere on a page.

How it works

  1. Click the Overlay Ink icon in the toolbar (or press Alt+Shift+O) on any page to open the popup. Turn On, then close it.
  2. Click anywhere on the page — the caret appears exactly where you clicked, and you type there. Every click starts a separate little text box, so your notes sit where you put them instead of running down the page in one column.
  3. Pick a colour, size and font in the extension popup. Select a note, then reopen the icon to change its style. Choose New notes instead in the popup to set defaults. Closing the popup never hides the canvas. Drag a note's ⠯ grip to move it, or its ✕ to delete it.
  4. Switch to View mode to keep everything on screen while you use the page normally. Esc first deselects a note, then switches to View.
  5. Switch Off in the popup, or press Alt+Shift+H, to hide it all. Your notes are kept and come back next time.

In Edit mode the page underneath stops responding to clicks so nothing moves while you write — that is deliberate. In View mode every click goes to the page as usual. There is no on-page control bar in either mode, so the whole screen is available for writing.

Defaults for new notes

Colour, size and font for the next note you write. Existing notes keep their own style — select one on the page and open the extension popup to change it.

Keyboard shortcuts

  • Alt+Shift+OOpen the extension popup (same as clicking the icon)
  • Alt+Shift+HHide the overlay while it is open
  • EscOn the page: deselect a note, then switch to View. In the popup: close controls only

Open brave://extensions/shortcuts and look for “Overlay Ink”.

Your data

Your notes and these settings are stored locally in your browser profile. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.

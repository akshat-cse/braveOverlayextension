Live demo · v0.3.0 · popup controls · click-anywhere canvas

Your whole page is the canvas.

Click the purple extension icon at the top right. A standard dropdown popup holds On/Off, Edit/View and all your writing controls. Turn the overlay on, then close the popup: nothing stays in the way of your notes.

Try the new popup

  1. Click the purple icon, switch On, and choose your colour, size and font.
  2. Choose Done · write on page, use the popup’s ✕, or click outside it. The popup closes, but the canvas stays on.
  3. Click anywhere and type there. Click another spot for a separate note. There is no floating bar and no reserved area at the top.
  4. To restyle a note, select it and reopen the icon. Choose New notes instead if you want to set defaults without changing that note.
  5. Reopen the popup for View or Off. Closing the popup never turns the overlay off.

Still your transparent canvas

  • Only your writing is drawn. The background starts at 0%, completely transparent.
  • Drag a note’s ⠯ grip to move it, or use its ✕ to delete it. These small handles only appear while editing.
  • Eight colours, a rainbow custom picker and a typed hex colour. Each note keeps its own colour, size and font.
  • Esc deselects a note; another Esc switches to View. Alt+Shift+H hides the overlay.

Edit to write. View to use the page.

Edit mode gives your clicks to the canvas so you can write without accidentally following a link. View mode makes every part of the overlay click-through: keep your notes visible and continue reading, scrolling or using the website underneath. There is no on-page control bar in either mode.

Switch to View in the popup, close it, then try the button or this ordinary page link.

Write without getting boxed in.

A reminder beside a paragraph, a question in the margin, a title in the corner. Each click places an independent note exactly where you want it. Reopening the popup reads your current settings and selection; your note stays selected even when you move from the page into the popup.

This test site loads the extension’s real canvas and its real popup, using a small local browser API bridge. In Brave, the icon lives in the browser toolbar and the popup closes normally when you click back onto the page. Browser pages such as brave://settings cannot host extensions.
